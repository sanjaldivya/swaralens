Swara sequence

How it maps: each pitch is measured in cents from your Sa and rounded to the nearest of the 12 swaras. Shuddha swaras are shown plain, komal swaras underlined (Re), tivra Ma with a tick (Ma), mandra saptak with a dot below and taar saptak with a dot above. Set Sa to the song's tonic; changing it relabels everything already captured. Pitch tracking follows one melody line, so a solo voice or instrument works best. With heavy accompaniment it follows whatever is loudest.
