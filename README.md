# Swara Lens

Swara Lens listens to a song and charts its notes as Indian swaras (Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa).

- Open a song file (MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG), use the microphone, or play the demo scale
- In a full song, follows the voice first, then wind instruments, then strings; drums and beats are ignored
- Line graph with one circle per note, labelled with its swara; pauses drop to a Silence line
- Current pitch, the song's scale (with harmonium Safed/Kaali name) and tempo in BPM
- A copyable swara sequence

**Open the app:** https://sanjaldivya.github.io/swaralens/

On iPhone or iPad, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
